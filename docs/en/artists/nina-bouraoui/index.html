Nina Bouraoui

Author

A writer. Her text is the source of Otages, the portrait of Sylvie Meyer — an “ordinary and extraordinary” woman who tips over in a single act.

A writer born in 1967, her work explores identity, desire and memory, between France and Algeria. Winner of the 2005 Prix Renaudot for Mes mauvaises pensées.

Her text is the source of Otages: the portrait of Sylvie Meyer, an “ordinary and extraordinary” woman who tips over in a single act, at once reprehensible and liberating.
