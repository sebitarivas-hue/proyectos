Richard Brunel

Stage director

A stage director, he created Otages at the Théâtre de la Croix-Rousse for the Opéra de Lyon Festival, the house he directs.

A stage director, he has led the Opéra national de Lyon from 2021 to 2026, after heading the Comédie de Valence. In theatre and opera alike, he stages a repertoire rooted in the questions of our time.

He created Otages at the Théâtre de la Croix-Rousse for the Opéra de Lyon Festival (2024), after the text by Nina Bouraoui.
