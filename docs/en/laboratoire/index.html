03 · Research & LIPS

Research, at the heart of the stage.

At STOPERA!, research happens in practice: on stage, with performers, around voice, body, listening and presence. It takes the form of a laboratory — a time for experimentation, co-writing and encounter between disciplines.

Transmission extends this research: STOPERA! mentors young artists, shares its practices and builds ties with performers, universities and partner venues, in France and internationally. Its international laboratory, LIPS, is its heart.

Four fields of research

Body & voice

Embodiment, vocal dramaturgies, performative writing and presence.

Technologies & creation

Artificial intelligence, electronic media, interactive systems and emerging tools — always in service of the artistic question.

New narratives

Fragmented storytelling, documentary forms, speculative dramaturgies and interdisciplinary writing.

New formats

Opera-film, immersive experiences, performative installations, hybrid stage forms and site-specific projects.

Innovation & programmes

  • Generative AI & moving image
  • Music creation & contemporary opera
  • Practice-based research
  • Interactive media & live electronics
  • University partnerships
  • European cooperations
  • LIPS — laboratory & transmission

Research, university and European partners: GRAME (CNCM), UNSAM (Buenos Aires), Pôle Pixel (Villeurbanne), the European Creator's Lab — and university partners in France and abroad.

03 · LIPS

An international laboratory of stage practices.

LIPS — Laboratoire In(ter)discipliné de Prototypes Scéniques — brings together eight to ten emerging artists from all disciplines at each edition: composition, stage direction, visual arts, theatre, dance. Created in 2019, it opens its studios, tools and research teams across three working sessions, mentored by established artists.

A space for exchange, experimentation and co-writing, where transmission happens through practice. Digital tools are common material, never an end.

Approach

  • Co-writing
  • Interdisciplinarity
  • Intermediality
  • Scenic & sound prototypes
  • Mentoring & transmission
  • Residency

Editions

  • Pôle PixelVilleurbanne
  • GRAME — CNCMLyon
  • UNSAMBuenos Aires

With GRAME (CNCM) and Pôle Pixel, as part of the European Creator's Lab. 2023 edition: “new immersive narratives.” The laboratory champions gender equality and environmentally responsible creation.
