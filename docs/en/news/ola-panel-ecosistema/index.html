Sebastián Rivas on the « Circulation Ecosystem » panel — 17th OLA Conference

Participation in the « Circulation Ecosystem: Festivals, theatres, companies, ideas and territories » panel at the 17th annual Ópera Latinoamérica Conference (Santiago, December 2024).

Alongside Ramiro Osorio (Teatro Mayor Julio Mario Santo Domingo, Colombia), Carmen Romero (Fundación Teatro a Mil, Chile), Chantal Signorio (Festival Puerto de Ideas, Chile) and moderated by José Luis Rivero (Auditorio de Tenerife, Spain), Sebastián Rivas explores circulation ecosystems for artworks — festivals, theatres, companies, ideas and territories — within the contemporary Latin American musical scene.
