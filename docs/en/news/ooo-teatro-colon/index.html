OOO premieres at the Teatro Colón

A post-human operatic environment by Emma Terno and Valentín Pelisch, OOO premiered at the Teatro Colón's CETC (Buenos Aires) in September 2025.

Born from a cooperation between GRAME — CNCM (Lyon) and the Teatro Colón's CETC, OOO imagines a world after humanity, where a houseplant and a defective bot converse among objects and ghosts. Praised by the Argentine press (Infobae, Clarín, Ópera Latinoamérica), the work now enters international diffusion with STOPERA!.
