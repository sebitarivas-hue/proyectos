STOPERA! opens its platform

An international platform for creation, production, research and transmission launches in Gentilly.

STOPERA! brings together artists, performers, researchers and cultural partners around a single question: how voice, body and sound become presence on stage. The platform unites creation, research and transmission, directed by Sebastian Rivas and supported by Georges Aperghis as honorary president.
