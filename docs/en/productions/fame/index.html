[FAM]E

Pop-Up Exploratoire #1, devised with GRAME: a recital in which percussionist Olivia Martin shapes, through contemporary creation, a sensitive path around the feminine — women's voice, their place, their presence. Hosted by Guillaume Kosmicki, this “for everyone” programme is as much a concert as a way of letting a voice be heard. Premiered at the Auditorium — Orchestre National de Lyon (Salle Proton).
