Insistir

A stage performance for a single performer, embodied by Nicola Beller Carbone and built around works by Georges Aperghis. In dialogue with a founding gesture — Susan Sontag staging Waiting for Godot in besieged Sarajevo (1993) — it questions presence in extreme conditions: voice as material, repetition as act, insistence as resistance. Premiere planned in Mexico City in 2026.
