input / body / output

A performance for solo conductor, sensors, spatialised sound and interactive video — subtitled “Conducting the Invisible.” Rut Schreiner's conducting gesture, captured in real time, generates, modulates and sculpts sound: the body becomes an instrument, conducting becomes musical material.
